About Us

KraftKit is your destination for high-quality arts, paints, and pottery supplies, for artists from beginners to professionals.

Our curated range includes acrylics, watercolors, pottery clay, glazes and essential tools, sourced from reputable brands that put quality and safety first.

Our mission is to foster a community where imagination thrives, with supplies, ideas and support for your creative journey. Not sure where to start? Take the questionnaire and we'll suggest a kit.
